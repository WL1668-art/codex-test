# Quality bar

## Per page

- The primary conclusion is apparent within roughly three seconds.
- The hierarchy has one visual focus.
- Title, body, source, and page marker are distinguishable.
- Images are proportionate and intentionally cropped.
- Text is readable at presentation distance.
- The page has an intentional amount of whitespace.
- The composition serves its message rather than reusing a generic card layout.

## Whole deck

- Pages belong to one visual system.
- Density, contrast, and visual emphasis have a deliberate sequence.
- The deck contains neither a sustained run of clone-like layouts nor an unexplained style break.
- A visual climax and a quieter reading moment appear where the narrative benefits from them.
