# Design system

Define the following before full-deck production:

- palette and contrast roles;
- typography hierarchy and fallback fonts;
- 16:9 grid, safe margins, and spacing scale;
- page-number and technical-label pattern;
- caption/source treatment;
- image cropping, evidence zoom, and annotation treatment.

The visual system may draw from editorial, cinematic, Swiss, minimal, scientific, consulting, brand, or another content-appropriate direction. Keep it responsive to the brief rather than fixing a house look.

## Evidence treatment

Do not shrink a dense source screenshot until it becomes illegible. When a page relies on evidence, use the smallest number of enlarged local crops that prove its conclusion. Pair each crop with a restrained label, highlight, or annotation. Keep raw source material out of the visual hierarchy unless it is itself the subject.

## Rhythm

Balance information-heavy pages with space, focal imagery, or concise transition pages. A deck should not look like a repeated template, but variation must still use the same token system.
